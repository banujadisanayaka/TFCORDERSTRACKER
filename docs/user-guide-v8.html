Access & Sign-In

How to get in

V8 uses Google account authentication — no passwords. Every user signs in with their Google account and requests access. The system owner approves who gets in and which roles they can use.

01
Sign in with Google
Open the app and tap "Sign in with Google". A popup opens asking you to choose or confirm your Google account. No password needed — Google handles the security.
02
Request Your Role
First-time users see a role selection screen. Tap the role(s) you need — Admin, Packing, Production, Vins, or Manja. Then tap "Request Access". Your request goes to the system owner.
03
Awaiting Approval
You'll see a "Pending Review" screen. The system owner (Banuja) will approve or reject your request. Once approved, refresh the app and you're in.
04
Select Your Role
After approval, you can choose from the roles you were granted. Each role opens a different interface. You can switch roles at any time using the "Roles" button in the top bar.
💡
Owner bypass: The system owner (Banuja Disanayaka) has automatic full access to all roles without needing approval — they are identified by their Google account email.
🔒
No passwords in V8: The old role passwords (TFC@123, tfc@123) from V6 are gone. All access is now managed through Google sign-in and the owner approval flow — more secure and easier to manage.

Daily Workflow

How a day runs

A standard operational day at TFC TTDI, from morning setup to final dispatch — every step tracked in the system.

01
Admin creates the daily production plan
Admin logs in with Google, selects Admin role. Goes to Dashboard → 📋 Productions tab, navigates to the current week, and creates plans for each day — adding products with kg quantities, packet counts, and linked recipes. Plans go live to the Production team instantly.
02
Admin creates Purchase Orders
Admin clicks "+ Create Order" in the sidebar. Selects Vins or Manja kitchen, fills in the PO name and date, adds items manually or by pasting a WhatsApp list for bulk parsing. Submits — order is now live for all roles.
03
Production team cooks the daily plan
Production logs in with their Google account, selects Production role. Sees the daily plan at the top of their queue with all items. Taps 📖 Recipe to view ingredients and preparation steps. Marks each item ✓ Complete when done. Can also tap + Log Extra to record any additional items cooked outside the plan.
04
Packing team processes each order item
Packing selects an order from the sidebar, sees all items with their quantities. Uses ✓ Mark as Packed for items ready to go. Sends items to production using → Prod (or swipe on mobile). Flags issues with ⚠ Short or ✕ OOS. Uses inline edit (✎) to record exact quantities and notes.
05
Production handles order batches
Production sees all items in "Cooking" status under their kitchen section. Multiple orders for the same product are merged into a Master Batch. When done, hits ✓ Batch Complete — Mark Ready to Pack — all linked order items update to Ready to Pack simultaneously. Packing team sees the update instantly.
06
Packing dispatches and sends WhatsApp update
Once items are packed, Packing hits 🚀 Confirm Dispatch. When the order is fully done, taps 📲 WhatsApp to auto-generate a formatted status message covering everything sent, in production, and any issues — ready to send to the client in one tap.

The system uses four Firestore collections with real-time listeners — all updates sync instantly across all connected devices without any page refresh.

  id · product · qty
  unit · status
  packedQty · notes
  batchId · updatedAt
}

weekOf · status · notes

  id · product · kgQty
  packetQty · recipeName
  recipeId · notes · status
}

🔥
Real-time listeners: Every Firestore collection uses onSnapshot() — meaning all connected devices receive updates the instant any change is written. No polling, no manual refresh.
